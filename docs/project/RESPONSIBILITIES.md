# Responsibilities

Named owners are assigned by the project owner. Until then, role accountability controls reviews.

| Area | Accountable role | Responsible role | Independent review required? |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Scope and primary research question | Project/research owner | Research lead | Yes, before study freeze |
| Architecture and toolchain | Tech lead | Implementer | Yes, for material ADRs |
| Data licenses and release rights | Data steward | Source owner | Yes |
| Company and fiscal registry | Data steward | Data engineer | Yes, sampled mappings |
| Taxonomy and annotations | NLP lead | Annotators/NLP engineer | Yes, locked test set |
| Financial mappings | Financial-data reviewer | Data engineer | Yes |
| Empirical specification | Research lead | Quantitative analyst | Yes, before freeze |
| Release | Project owner | Tech/research leads | Yes, clean reproduction |

One person may hold several roles, but the locked NLP test set, fiscal-mapping sample, primary specification, and headline-result audit require a second reviewer.
